09.1Migration principles

  1. Deploy is not authority. Start and validate new code before any producer lease.
  2. Authority is scoped. Capsule, symbol, side, account, risk cell, arm and validity are explicit; no wildcard first activation.
  3. One writer. A new producer cannot acquire scope until the prior token is revoked/fenced and outstanding ownership is safe.
  4. Exit authority survives entry rollback. Cancels, protection, reconciliation and verified exposure reduction remain available.
  5. Evidence is append-only. A rollback does not delete losing events, failed trials or incident facts.
  6. Rollback contains risk first. Diagnose after entry authority is stopped and positions/orders/protection are known.
  7. No big bang. Contracts, transport, candidates, execution and economic authority migrate separately.
  8. No simultaneous expansion. Side, symbol, capsule, risk and universe move one dimension at a time.

09.3Cutover preflight package

Required signed artifacts

  • current and candidate build/artifact/SBOM digests;
  • contract, compatibility, config, risk, exit, transport, treatment, activation and rollback manifests;
  • exact scope: capsule, parameter set, symbol, side, account, position mode, risk cell and start/expiry;
  • successful gate receipts G−1 through the required current gate;
  • producer lease scope and next fencing token;
  • on-call roster, incident channel, risk/execution/security/operations approvers;
  • current venue snapshot: balances, position mode, positions, regular orders, algo orders, protections, reservations and account-stream health;
  • loss/anomaly/emergency/latency/coverage budgets and automatic actions;
  • last-known-good control manifest and rollback rehearsal receipt.

Go/no-go questions

  1. Are all required Binance streams on correct routes and READY?
  2. Is the current/private account truth reconciled and protection capacity available?
  3. Does ORIGIN match the candidate manifest and replay checksum exactly?
  4. Does E09 accept the prospective token and reject a stale test token?
  5. Can the canary scope be disabled without disabling exits?
  6. Is every expected event visible in strict money/evidence surfaces?
  7. Is the rollback owner present and authorized?
  8. Is there any unrelated deployment/config/trial change? If yes: NO-GO.

A preflight answer may be PASS, FAIL or INSUFFICIENT. Unknown is NO-GO.

09.4Connected dark and paired-comparison runbook

  1. Deploy ORIGIN under dedicated identity with no venue gateway path, risk/command topic ACL or money writer DSN.
  2. Load the signed dark activation manifest; verify ALLOW_MONEY_PUBLISH=false is enforced by capability/ACL, not merely a boolean.
  3. Start from a certified checkpoint or replay E01 state from declared warm-up offset.
  4. Publish startup/readiness/attestation; do not request candidate authority lease.
  5. Feed the identical market_state.v2 offsets to frozen legacy bridge and ORIGIN.
  6. Record all atoms, transitions, reactions, candidates, withdrawals, heartbeats and named zero reasons.
  7. Comparator writes divergence records; classify every difference against a requirement, known legacy defect, or new defect.
  8. Continuously test that ORIGIN has zero E07-selected events, risk requests, authorizations, commands and orders unless a separate non-money treatment test explicitly exists.
  9. At the frozen end boundary, seal inputs/outputs/config and produce prospective evidence receipt. No threshold changes while the window is open.
Dark success is necessary, not economic proof. It proves causality, coverage, deterministic behavior, resource safety and prospective signal behavior under a fill model. It cannot prove actual maker fills or net money EV.

09.5Isolated canary runbook

Scope law

One capsule × one symbol × one side certification × one isolated live account/risk cell × minimum practical notional × one producer lease. The opposite side requires its own evidence; multiple entry is disabled.

Activation sequence

  1. Freeze candidate/control builds and manifests; open a signed canary event.
  2. Verify account is in the expected one-way/hedge mode and all positions/orders/protection are known.
  3. Set control/treatment allocation prospectively and mutually exclusively.
  4. Issue treatment candidate lease for exact scope; E07 confirms one selected candidate at most.
  5. E08 uses reduced canary risk and absolute loss/anomaly budgets; E09 validates GTX, filters, protection capacity and strict lineage.
  6. Before the first opportunity, inject a stale-token command and prove rejection without venue effect.
  7. For each opportunity, trace candidate→decision→risk→reservation→authorization→command→order→fill→position→protection→outcome or named terminal refusal.
  8. Monitor stop conditions continuously. Automatic stop disables new entries first and starts the appropriate rollback path.
  9. End only at preregistered boundary or safety stop. Seal all evidence before reading economic result.

Required live dashboard, separately labeled

feed/route/quality, ORIGIN watermark/checksum/lease, control/treatment allocation, complete funnel, order/liquidity/partials/rejects/unknowns, positions/protection age, reservations, strict v3 completeness, fees/funding/net P&L, maker markouts, emergency events, loss/anomaly budget and rollback readiness.

09.6Scoped authority cutover runbook

A · Freeze

  1. Freeze legacy detector changes except safety fixes and freeze ORIGIN semantic/config bytes.
  2. Snapshot source commits, artifacts, manifests, ledger offsets, account state and producer tokens.
  3. Announce exact scope and no-change window.

B · Drain legacy scope

  1. Disable legacy new entries for the scope while preserving cancels/protection/exits.
  2. Cancel stale legacy entry orders.
  3. Wait for campaigns to become flat, or record explicit per-campaign owner through completion. ORIGIN never inherits implicitly.
  4. Reconcile regular orders, Algo orders, fills, positions, protection and E08 reservations.

C · Prepare ORIGIN without authority

  1. Start green ORIGIN on production inputs; verify input offsets/hashes and warm/current state.
  2. Verify candidate output/dark comparator and that an attempted authoritative write is rejected.
  3. Verify E07/E08/E09 consumers have the exact contract/config/activation bundle.

D · Transfer fenced authority

  1. Revoke legacy producer lease and persist revocation.
  2. Advance fencing token; test legacy rejection at E07 and any downstream boundary.
  3. Issue ORIGIN lease for exact scope; validate ORIGIN token and activation manifest.
  4. Record promotion event and enable prospective allocation only.

E · Observe and close

  1. Watch all automatic stop conditions through the ratified stabilization window.
  2. Compare control observation and ORIGIN on same inputs; report every divergence/side effect.
  3. Close the cutover receipt only after no ambiguous order/position/protection/reservation remains.

09.7Rollback runbooks

Case 1 — dark ORIGIN, no authority

  1. Stop ORIGIN input or process.
  2. Seal checkpoint/output/divergence evidence.
  3. Confirm no authority/money journal event exists.
  4. Legacy/control path is unchanged; diagnose offline.

Case 2 — authority issued, no open order or position

  1. Set allocation to zero and revoke ORIGIN candidate lease.
  2. Prove consumers reject token.
  3. Reconcile zero orders/positions/reservations for scope.
  4. If desired, issue a strictly higher legacy/control token under last-known-good manifest.
  5. Record rollback event and preserve ORIGIN evidence.

Case 3 — open/unknown order, no confirmed position

  1. Disable new ORIGIN entries and revoke candidate authority; E09 retains cancel/reconcile authority.
  2. Search private stream and query venue by client/economic ID; do not blindly resubmit or assume absent.
  3. Cancel known open entry order; account for late fills and re-evaluate case if a position exists.
  4. Release E08 reservation only after order absence/terminal state is authoritative.
  5. Only then restore control authority.

Case 4 — open or partial position

  1. Disable new entries and fence ORIGIN candidate authority. Do not disable protection/cancel/reduction.
  2. Verify position, regular/algo orders, protection coverage, risk reservation and account stream.
  3. Assign one explicit campaign owner: normally E09 under the originating authorization/policy until flat.
  4. Follow normal maker exit while safe; invoke governed emergency exit if protection/urgency thresholds require.
  5. Reconcile venue position to zero, cancel orphan orders/protection and release reservation exactly once.
  6. Only after flat proof may legacy/control reacquire a new token. Never let two engines own the same position.

Case 5 — post-cutover systemic defect

  1. Revoke affected scopes and halt widening globally; preserve healthy exit paths.
  2. Rollback one scope at a time using the cases above.
  3. Restore last-known-good artifacts/manifests only after schema/data compatibility check.
  4. Rebuild projections from immutable facts if necessary; never delete losing/defect evidence.
  5. Open incident and new trial/build identity before attempting repromotion.
Forbidden rollback shortcut. Do not reactivate legacy immediately while ORIGIN orders, positions, protection or reservations are ambiguous. That creates dual ownership and can duplicate or reverse exposure.

09.9Incident command flow

  1. Detect: automatic guard emits immutable incident trigger with account/scope/evidence.
  2. Contain: revoke entry authority and allocation; preserve exits, protection and raw evidence.
  3. Establish truth: reconcile route health, producer tokens, orders, fills, positions, Algo protection and reservations from venue/raw facts.
  4. Reduce risk: repair protection or follow approved emergency reduction policy.
  5. Stabilize: reach known flat/protected state and seal ledgers/checkpoints.
  6. Recover: select last-known-good manifest, validate compatibility, issue new fenced authority only after go/no-go.
  7. Analyze: reconstruct exact causal timeline from source/receipt/knowledge/dispatch/venue clocks; identify guard/test gap.
  8. Correct: new requirement/test/build/trial where semantic; no in-place evidence-window change.
  9. Review: independent risk/security/architecture sign-off before repromotion.

Manual action is recorded with operator identity, reason, evidence, exact payload/result and two-person approval where required. Operators never paste credentials into incident records.

09.10Disaster recovery and restore

Protected artifacts

Raw ledgers, contract/config/activation/rollback manifests, producer-lease log, money journals, ORIGIN journals/checkpoints, instrument/fee revisions, trial registry, test/gate receipts and audit/incident records.

Restore order

  1. Verify sealed segment/checkpoint hashes and restore into an isolated environment.
  2. Restore contract/config/control-plane state; do not restore active leases as active.
  3. Restore E09 account truth from venue plus ledger; reconcile before entry.
  4. Restore E00/E01 and rebuild canonical state to current watermark.
  5. Restore ORIGIN checkpoint, replay suffix and compare checksum.
  6. Restore E07/E08 projections/reservations; unresolved money state remains fail-closed.
  7. Start all candidates in READY_NO_AUTHORITY; issue fresh higher fencing tokens only after full readiness.

DR proof

Quarterly/cadence decision remains TBD, but before production cutover at least one full restore and one corrupted/missing-checkpoint rebuild must pass with declared recovery point/time objectives. RPO/RTO values require owner ratification; no invented numbers appear here.

09.11Progressive scale and legacy retirement

Expansion order

  1. Opposite side for the already certified capsule/symbol.
  2. Next symbol for the same capsule at unchanged risk.
  3. Next isolated capsule on already understood symbols.
  4. Risk allocation increase only after execution capacity/tail evidence.
  5. Universe expansion last.

Every step freezes one new activation manifest, has its own loss/anomaly budget and gate receipt, and preserves a matched control where practicable. A positive aggregate cannot certify a negative/underpowered side or symbol.

Legacy removal criteria

  • no active writer lease or ACL;
  • no open campaign/order/protection/reservation owned by legacy;
  • no current consumer needs legacy schema/topic/bridge;
  • rollback window expired under approved retention;
  • final parity/divergence and deployment receipts archived;
  • runbooks/configs/dashboards no longer advertise legacy as authority;
  • service/process and credentials removed, not merely disabled.

09.13Required immutable receipts

  • Baseline receipt: repos/artifacts/processes/config/contracts/accounts/writers/tapes.
  • Security receipt: token rotation, secret scan, process-environment and ACL evidence.
  • Replay receipt: exact inputs/fidelity/build/manifests/checkpoints/output hashes/tests.
  • Dark receipt: live offsets, zero authority/side effects, coverage/SLO/divergence/prospective evidence.
  • Execution rehearsal receipt: all venue/Algo/unknown/emergency/reconciliation fixtures.
  • Preflight receipt: go/no-go answers, venue/account snapshot, owners and budgets.
  • Authority transfer receipt: old/new tokens, revocation/acceptance proofs, exact scope and UTC events.
  • Canary/economic receipt: allocation, campaigns, costs, stop events, statistical boundary and conclusion class.
  • Rollback receipt: trigger, revoked scope, order/position/protection/reservation truth, restored manifest and residual risk.
  • Retirement receipt: zero consumers/writers/credentials and archived retention artifacts.
