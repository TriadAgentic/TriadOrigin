00.1Executive control decision

BUILD

Clean deterministic E02 replacement

Build ORIGIN inside the existing TRIAD chassis. Do not create another E00–E10 monolith and do not copy any legacy detector wholesale. Reuse only independently verified contracts, venue mechanics, exact arithmetic, safety rails, and operational patterns.

PROVE

Causality, attribution, then economics

Ingress, exact replay, identity, fill lineage, position truth, and rollback are blocking gates. A safety canary proves operational safety; only an adequately powered, preregistered money sample can support an economic claim.

No profitability claim. The current money dataset cannot attribute exact net EV to deterministic structures, and the prior shadow and money populations have no scorecard overlap. ORIGIN is an engineering and research specification; profitability remains an empirical hypothesis.
Approved execution posture. Normal entries and exits are post-only maker. Taker execution is allowed only as an E09-owned, governed, position-reducing emergency escape. It may never open, add, or reverse exposure.

00.4Authority and normative precedence

If two artifacts disagree, resolve the conflict in this order:

1 · Owner decisionAuthenticated, scoped decision record
2 · MasterNormative architecture and laws
3 · SchemasApproved contract bytes and manifest
4 · State catalogTransition tables and invariants
5 · WiringProducer/consumer and delivery semantics
6 · RunbooksOperational procedures

Examples, diagrams, dashboards, generated clients, comments, and prior reports are non-authoritative. They MUST NOT override a schema or normative requirement. This RC1 is a ratification candidate; it does not itself arm a service, alter a live account, or authorize a production deployment.

Normative language

MUST / MUST NOT / SHALL are release-blocking. SHOULD / SHOULD NOT require a recorded exception with owner, rationale, scope, expiry, and compensating control. MAY is optional. TBD is not permission to choose locally; the dependent path remains blocked.

Security release blocker

A source audit reported that the private startup script globally sources live.env, causing broad secret inheritance, and contains a literal MCP bearer token. The token value is deliberately excluded here. Required action: rotate the exposed credential, purge it from active files and history according to incident policy, replace global environment inheritance with per-service least-privilege injection, and add secret scanning. Deployment remains blocked until independently verified.

Contract correction

Contracts release 1.37.0 already contains fill.v2, but only a small core is required and most economic lineage is optional. ORIGIN therefore specifies fill.v3 as a new strict contract. structure_atom.v1 contains event/confirmation/available times but lacks the full stable identity, tick geometry, quality, and append-only lifecycle required here; it is superseded on the ORIGIN route by structure_atom.v2.

00.9Change control, ratification, and signatures

Required change sequence

  1. Open an immutable change proposal with affected requirements, contracts, tests, rollout scope, risk, and rollback.
  2. Assign a new trial identity for any economic-semantic change.
  3. Update the normative source, then regenerate schemas, clients, examples, checklists, and documentation.
  4. Run compatibility, determinism, safety, and affected economic gates.
  5. Obtain role approvals; sign the contract/config/build manifest.
  6. Deploy dark; canary only under a scoped producer lease; record the promotion event.

Emergency changes MAY reduce exposure or disable entries. They MUST NOT broaden money authority, enable a new detector, or relax a risk boundary. An emergency change expires and receives retrospective review.
