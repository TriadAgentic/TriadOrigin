10.7Decision lifecycle

  1. Propose: immutable decision ID, owner, alternatives, evidence, affected requirements/contracts/tests/trials, risk and rollback.
  2. Review: architecture, risk, execution, data/security and evidence roles according to scope.
  3. Ratify or reject: authenticated owner decision with exact scope and UTC time.
  4. Implement: new contract/config/build/manifest bytes; no silent reinterpretation.
  5. Verify: affected checklist and tests plus compatibility/rollback.
  6. Activate: signed manifest and producer lease; decision alone never self-deploys.
  7. Observe: metrics and automatic stop; exceptions expire.
  8. Supersede: later ADR references prior; prior remains in audit history.
